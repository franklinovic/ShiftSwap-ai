# Project Charter: ShiftSwap

## Project Purpose
Provide hourly employees with a simple way to offer shifts they cannot work and request open shifts from coworkers, while ensuring a manager approves each proposed swap before it becomes final.

## Objectives
- Deliver a demonstrable MVP in one two-week Sprint 1.
- Enable employees to post a shift for coverage, browse available shifts, and request a shift.
- Give managers a clear way to review, approve, or reject requests, with the outcome visible to the affected employees.
- Validate the core workflow with Operations, HR, and frontline employee representatives before the sprint ends.

## Stakeholders & Roles
- **Operations Manager — Business owner:** Prioritizes the workflow and confirms operational fit.
- **HR Director — Policy stakeholder:** Reviews alignment with applicable workforce policies and approval expectations.
- **Dev Lead — Delivery lead:** Guides technical decisions, implementation, and sprint delivery.
- **Frontline employees — End users:** Validate usability and use the app to offer and request shifts.
- **Project team — Delivery:** Designs, builds, and tests the MVP with stakeholder feedback.

## Success Criteria
- A frontline employee can post a shift, another employee can request it, and the responsible manager can approve or reject the request.
- A swap is not shown as final until manager approval; the decision is visible to the affected employees.
- Operations, HR, and employee representatives review a working end-to-end demonstration by the end of Sprint 1.
- No critical defects prevent completion of the core offer–request–approval workflow during the final sprint review.

## High-Level Timeline
**One two-week Sprint 1:** Week 1 — confirm workflow and policy assumptions, design, and implement the core offer/request flow. Week 2 — complete manager approval and status visibility, test the end-to-end MVP, gather stakeholder feedback, and demonstrate at sprint review.

## Assumptions & Constraints
- Sprint 1 is limited to two weeks and focuses on a usable MVP of the offer–request–manager approval workflow.
- The responsible manager and applicable approval rules can be identified for each request.
- For this draft, the MVP assumes basic employee and manager access and status notifications within the app; identity-provider, scheduling/payroll, and external notification integrations are out of scope unless prioritized by stakeholders.
- HR and Operations will clarify applicable eligibility, working-time, and approval policies during the sprint; the app does not replace those policies.
- Scope, team capacity, and stakeholder availability are limited to the agreed Sprint 1 backlog and review window.
