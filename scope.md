# ShiftSwap — MVP Scope

## In Scope (MVP)
- Employees can post a shift they need covered, including the shift details needed for coworkers and a manager to assess the request.
- Employees can browse open shifts and request to pick up a shift that fits.
- The responsible manager can review a request and approve or reject it; a shift change is not final until approval.
- Employees can see request status and the manager’s decision in the app.
- A basic employee/manager experience supports the offer–request–approval workflow, with the workflow validated by stakeholders during Sprint 1.

## Out of Scope (v1)
- **Payroll or workforce-scheduling system integration:** Deferred to keep Sprint 1 focused on the core workflow and avoid dependencies on external systems, data mapping, and reconciliation.
- **Single sign-on (SSO) and enterprise identity provisioning:** Deferred because authentication integration adds organizational setup and security review beyond what is needed to validate the MVP workflow.
- **Third-party notifications (such as email or SMS):** Deferred to avoid vendor configuration, delivery costs, and notification preferences before the team validates which events and channels employees need. Request status is available in the app.

## Sprint 1 Goal
By the end of the two-week Sprint 1, demonstrate an end-to-end MVP in which an employee posts a shift needing coverage, a coworker requests it, and the responsible manager approves or rejects the request before any swap is treated as final.
